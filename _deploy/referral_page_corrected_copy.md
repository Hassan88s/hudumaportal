## 🇬🇧 English Version

### Hero (Section Title)
**Unlock Opportunities with HudumaPortal!**

### Intro Paragraph
Refer friends to Huduma Portal and earn real rewards when they join and actively use the platform. Downloads alone or unverified accounts do NOT qualify — only real, completed activity earns you money.

### How It Works (3 steps)

**1. Get Your Referral Link**
Once you register on Huduma Portal, you receive a personal referral link in your Earn dashboard. This link is unique to you and tracks all your referrals automatically.

**2. Share Your Link**
Send your link to friends, colleagues, or anyone who might benefit from freelancing or hiring on Huduma Portal. Share via WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, email, or any personal channel — as long as it follows our rules and Tanzanian laws.

**3. They Sign Up + Take Action**
When someone uses your link to join Huduma Portal, they'll need to create AND verify their account. Rewards unlock as they complete real activity — signing up alone is not enough.

### Reward Breakdown

**Referring a Freelancer (Provider) — earn up to TZS 3,000**
- Stage 1: Freelancer publishes their first service → **TZS 500**
- Stage 2: Freelancer receives their first paid order → **TZS 1,000** (plus TZS 1,000 credit for the new freelancer)
- Stage 3: Freelancer receives a second order OR buys a paid subscription → **TZS 1,500**

**Referring a Client (Buyer) — earn up to TZS 1,500**
- Welcome: The new client gets **TZS 1,000 credit** for their first booking
- Stage 1: Client completes their first booking → **TZS 750**
- Stage 2: Client completes a second booking within 60 days → **TZS 750**

### What Doesn't Qualify (per our Terms)
- ❌ App or website downloads alone
- ❌ Unverified sign-ups (email/phone must be verified)
- ❌ Free service posts with no paid activity
- ❌ Self-referrals or fake accounts
- ❌ Same phone, email domain, IP, or payment account used across multiple "referrals"

### How You Get Paid
1. Rewards land in your **Referral Wallet** as **PENDING**
2. After a **14-day protection window** (to cover refunds), rewards become **AVAILABLE**
3. **Transfer** the available balance from your Referral Wallet to your Main Wallet (minimum TZS 5,000)
4. **Withdraw** from your Main Wallet as normal — M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Mix by Yas, HaloPesa, NMB, CRDB, and more

## 🗒️ FAQ Block — Corrected Answers

### 1. Who qualifies as a "referred user"?
Only NEW users who have never registered on Huduma Portal before, who use your referral link OR enter your code within 24 hours of signing up, AND who verify their phone or email.

### 2. What activity earns rewards?
For **freelancers**: publishing their first service, receiving their first paid order, and receiving a second order or paid subscription.
For **clients**: completing their first paid booking and a second within 60 days.
Downloads, browsing, and unverified sign-ups do not earn rewards.

### 3. When do rewards become available?
Rewards start as PENDING and become AVAILABLE after a 14-day protection window. This gives us time to handle any refunds that might reverse the reward.

### 4. What is the minimum I can transfer to my Main Wallet?
TZS 5,000. Below this, rewards stay in your Referral Wallet until you accumulate enough.

### 5. Can I refer myself with a different phone number?
No. Self-referrals are automatically blocked. Duplicate accounts (same phone prefix, email pattern, payment account, or device) are flagged and rejected.

### 6. Are there any per-day or per-user limits?
Yes. Rewards are capped per referred user: TZS 3,000 max for a provider, TZS 1,500 max for a client, TZS 10,000 max for a business.

### 7. How do I get paid?
Transfer approved rewards from your Referral Wallet to your Main Wallet, then withdraw from your Main Wallet using any supported payout method (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Mix by Yas, HaloPesa, NMB, CRDB, etc.).

### 8. What happens if my referred user gets a refund?
If a refund happens within the 14-day protection window, the reward is reversed and marked "rejected". After the protection window, rewards are safe.

### 9. Can I appeal a rejected referral?
Yes — contact support with the referred user's name and referral ID. Admin can review IP, device, and activity records.

### 10. Where do I see my referrals and earnings?
In the **Earn (Refer & Rewards)** tab in your dashboard — includes your link, referred users, milestone progress, reward events, and transfer button.
